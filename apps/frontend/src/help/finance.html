Finance Guide

Track Income, Expenses, and Pay-Period Profit

The Finance Manager is designed for short-cycle earnings tracking with automated commission splits, tax estimates, itemized expense management, and a persistent pay-period overview.

What This Page Is For

Use this page to record your daily earnings, manage personal or business expenses, and keep an accurate, real-time track of your 14-day pay-period progress.

Understanding the Form Fields

  • Date: The day this entry corresponds to. You can backdate entries or edit existing ones easily by changing this date.
  • Service Sales ($): Total basic sales generated from services. This forms the primary base of your commissions.
  • Cash Tips ($): Tips received in physical cash. This is kept entirely by you and added directly to your Net Payout with 0% tax deducted here.
  • CC Tips ($): Tips received through credit card payments. This is added to your Commission Base and paid out through commissions.
  • Entry Description / Notes: Custom notes to document events of the day (e.g., weather conditions, holiday shift information, or customer patterns).
  • Daily Expenses: Itemized costs incurred during the day (e.g., fuel, lunch, supplies). Click + Add Expense to add a cost item.

Financial Calculations & Commission Splits

The app automatically applies a professional split and tax algorithm to all your entries:

  1. Commission Base: The total amount eligible for commission payouts. Calculated as: Commission Base = (Service Sales × 60%) + CC Tips
  2. Check Commission: The portion of commissions paid out via check or direct deposit. Set at 60% of the Commission Base: Check Commission = Commission Base × 60%
  3. Cash Commission: The portion of commissions paid out directly in cash. Set at 40% of the Commission Base: Cash Commission = Commission Base × 40%
  4. Tax Deductions (15%): Estimated income tax withheld automatically at 15% from the Check Commission: Tax Amount = Check Commission × 15%
  5. Net Paycheck (Net Check): The actual check payout you take home after taxes have been deducted: Net Paycheck = Check Commission - Tax Amount

Understanding the Side Summary (Pay-Period KPIs)

The persistent right panel accumulates all entries made during your active 14-day cycle:

  • Period Progress: Shows the timeline elapsed in your standard 14-day pay period. Click Start New Period to close the current cycle and start fresh.
  • Gross (Total Earnings): All money earned before taxes and expenses: Gross = Commission Base + Cash Tips.
  • Tax (15%): Total estimated taxes accumulated over the period.
  • Net Payout: Your actual, real take-home payout in your hand: Net Payout = Cash Commission + Net Paycheck + Cash Tips.
  • Expenses: Total combined daily expenses recorded in this pay-period.
  • Real Profit: The ultimate measure of your cycle's profitability: Real Profit = Net Payout - Expenses. Dynamically changes to Green (profit) or Red (loss).

Editing & Searching History

  • Collapsible History Drawer: Under the tab, click on any history row to expand detailed daily earnings, check payouts, and view itemized expenses.
  • Date & Note Editing: Expand a history drawer and click ✏️ Edit this Entry to load all information back into the form. You can modify any number and even edit the entry date! The system automatically cleans up old entries on date changes.
  • Search Query Filter: Use the search box to find specific entries immediately. Type any description, category, specific day, or money amount to filter.
  • Monthly Filter: Choose a dynamic month option from the dropdown menu to isolate records for that month instantly.
