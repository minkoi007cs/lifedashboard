Finance Guide

Track Income, Expenses, Taxes, and Balance

The Finance Manager records the money you actually receive: check income before tax, cash income that is already yours, estimated tax on check income, and daily expenses. Money values are displayed with US-style grouping and two decimals, for example 1,000.00$.

What This Page Is For

Use this page to record daily income and expenses, then review check income, cash income, total income, taxes, expenses, and balance or shortfall by month, rolling period, year, or all time.

Daily Entry Form

  • Date: The day this entry belongs to. You can backdate entries or edit existing ones.
  • Description / Notes: One shared note for the whole daily entry. Income and expense no longer have separate descriptions.
  • Income / Expense tabs: The money input area is compact. Use the Income tab for received money and the Expense tab for money spent.
  • Income Check - Before Tax ($): Check-paid income after commission has already been applied, before tax. Previous Service Sales entries are treated as this value.
  • Income Cash ($): Cash income already received after commission. It is not taxed in this tracker. Previous Cash Tips entries are treated as this value.
  • Tips: Tips are not special fields anymore. Add them as normal check or cash income and write Tip in the description.
  • Expense ($): Daily money spent. The expense reuses the shared description unless no description is entered.

Financial Calculations

The app no longer calculates commission and no longer uses pay-period entry flows. It only estimates tax on check income:

  1. Gross Income: Total income before tax and expenses: Gross Income = Income Check + Income Cash
  2. Tax Estimate: Estimated tax on check income only: Tax = Income Check x 15%
  3. Net Income: Income after estimated tax: Net Income = Income Check - Tax + Income Cash
  4. Balance: Net income after expenses: Balance = Net Income - Expenses

Dashboard KPIs

  • Check Income: Check-paid income before estimated tax.
  • Cash Income: Cash received directly with no tax deducted by this tracker.
  • Total Income: Check plus cash before tax.
  • Expenses: Daily expenses for the selected period.
  • Taxes: Estimated 15% tax on check income.
  • Balance / Shortfall: Net income after tax minus expenses.
  • Remembered selections: Each KPI remembers its selected period in the browser.

Finance Report Charts

  • Income, Expense & Tax Report: Shows check income and cash income stacked together, with expense and tax beside income for comparison.
  • Balance Trend: Shows total income, expense, tax, and balance as separate trend lines.
  • Expense Breakdown: Shows expense distribution by category.
  • Chart duration: Each report chart has its own period selector: this month, last month, last 30 days, last 90 days, this year, last year, or all time.
  • Report grouping: Periods shorter than one year show day-by-day data. Yearly and all-time reports show week-by-week data.

History, Search, and Export

  • Collapsible History Drawer: Click any history row to review daily income, tax, net income, balance, and itemized expenses.
  • Date & Note Editing: Expand a history drawer and click Edit this Entry to load the data back into the form.
  • Search Query Filter: Search by description, category, date, or money amount.
  • Monthly Filter: Choose a month from the dropdown to isolate records for that month.
  • CSV Export: Exports check income, cash income, tax, gross income, net income after tax, expenses, balance, description, and expense details.

Finance Sharing

You can share your Finance with other users — for example, a partner or accountant — and choose what level of access they have.

Inviting someone

  1. Click the Share button in the top-right area of the Finance page.
  2. In the Share Access tab, enter the account email of the person you want to invite.
  3. Choose View only or Can edit, then click Send Invite.
  4. The invite will appear as Pending until the other person accepts it. You can revoke it at any time.
  5. If you send a new invite to someone who already has access, their permission level is updated instead of creating a duplicate.

Accepting or rejecting an invite

  1. When someone invites you, a red badge appears on the Share button.
  2. Open the modal and go to the Invites Received tab.
  3. Click the green check to Accept or the red circle to Reject.

Switching to another user's Finance

  1. After accepting an invite, click the context switcher dropdown (shows My Finance by default) next to the Share button.
  2. Select the person whose finance you want to view. A blue banner appears confirming whose data you are viewing and your access level.
  3. To return to your own data, click Back to My Finance in the banner or select My Finance from the dropdown.

Access levels

  • View only: You can browse the dashboard and scroll through history, but the entry form and edit/delete buttons are hidden.
  • Can edit: You have full access to create, edit, and delete entries as if it were your own finance.

Revoking access

  • As the owner: Open Share → Share Access tab → click the trash icon next to any person to remove their access immediately.
  • As the recipient: Open Share → Invites Received tab → click Leave next to an accepted share to remove yourself.

A user can invite multiple people, and a single user can accept invites from multiple owners. Only one person's finance is shown at a time — switch using the context dropdown.
